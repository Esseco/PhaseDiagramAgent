# MC energy provenance and same-frame correction

The MC search ranks candidates by the committee mean, while ordinary model-refresh and Relax energies use the designated main model. Historical MC outputs from the previous worker may therefore have the same `model_version` as primary-model energies but a different energy basis. QBC `member_count` alone does not establish the basis: model-refresh predictions compute committee statistics while publishing primary-model energy.

The MC worker now retains per-member energies from the matching in-memory selected pool evaluation, verifies its step, member count, finiteness and mean, and publishes the main member's energy for the selected frame. It preserves the committee mean, per-member values and force-disagreement evidence. Multi-model output fails closed if the selected-frame member energy cannot be verified. This does not alter MC ranking or historical outputs. Input preparation defers per-batch archive and index work and reuses its private state; owned phase identification avoids redundant copies while retaining geometry checks.

A guard rejects DFT and MC planning when a saved hull is demonstrably built with legacy MC committee means: it matches each hull record to its source task and verifies the saved energy equals the ranked committee mean. Absence of proof is not proof of consistency; this guard is not a complete energy-basis audit. It never overwrites historical data.

NaCo batch `relax-mc-b1e3f0ff93f892df` has 146 returned final frames, including 135 in-boundary records that entered hull `3216bbb4d795` using committee means. The hull must not drive further selection. A read-only 146-frame candidate manifest is saved in the Codex workspace as `naco_mc_main_energy_correction_review.json`; it binds each frame hash, task, original model and version. It is not a project-approved task or submission. An Agent-owned, scientifically reviewed same-frame prediction and recovery path is still needed to obtain verified main-model energies, preserve the originals, rebuild the hull, and then evaluate DFT versus exploration. Remote traces contain matching four-member trial energies for 99 selected nonzero-step frames, while the remaining 47 selected step-zero frames lack verified selected-frame vectors in the examined trace; means alone cannot recover individual members. Full uniform predictions may be simpler to validate than a mixed provenance repair. No such jobs have been generated or submitted.

Measurements and tests establish avoided copies and valid semantic behavior, not a guaranteed end-to-end runtime; history revalidation and remote resources remain variable. Existing running services do not automatically load new source.
